MotionPR is created and maintained by Eren Sümer. The people below have contributed to it. Thank
you.

## Author and maintainer

- **Eren Sümer** ([@erenthedeveloper0](https://github.com/erenthedeveloper0)) — creator of

## Contributors

Contributions of every size are listed here: code, documentation, adapters, bug reports that came
with a reproduction. To add yourself, include a line in this section in your pull request, in the
same format:

```
- **Your Name** ([@github-handle](https://github.com/github-handle)) — what you worked on
```

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for how to get started.
